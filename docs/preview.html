Context Canopy

Design mock-ups of the band in different sessions: different plans, from no agents to a deep tree, near the limit, narrow terminals and collapsed. Screenshots drawn from the plugin’s own layout code are in the README.

Reading the band

The two colour sets never overlap. Model colours say who, health colours say how full. Health colours apply to context fill and to the percentage figures on the plan line.

Health

  • ▆▆Quality starts to slip, 60 to 89% of the way
  • ▆▆Compaction imminent, 90% of the way or more

Agent status

  • ●Running
  • ◐Waiting (on a tool, a limit or permission)
  • ○Idle teammate
  • ◇Teammate in its own pane, no context data
  • ✗Failed or stopped

States

Each mock-up shows the end of the transcript, the band, the prompt box and your existing status line beneath it. [-] is the engine's collapse mark. ↻ is time until the window resets. The ≈ on the plan line marks the model split as an estimate.

Build steps

Steps 1 to 6 are built and tested. Step 7 needs a real session on your machine, and step 8 is partly done.

Decided

  1. The band shows all the time, so plan and session usage stay in view even with no agents running.
  2. Health is tied to the auto-compact point. Amber from 60% of the way there, where long contexts start to lose quality, and red from 90%, when compaction is imminent. Both are settings. The percentage shown is of the full window, to match /context.
  3. The plan model split is on by default, marked ≈.
  4. Model colours stay as shown.
  5. Finished agents drop off the moment they finish.
